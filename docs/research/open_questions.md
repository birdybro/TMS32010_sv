# Open architecture questions

Open questions are not implementation permissions. Provisional choices require
a targeted test and must retain their confidence label.

| ID | Question | Status / competing hypotheses | Evidence needed | Hard Drivin' impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OQ-001 | Can external cycles be lengthened on a part with no READY pin? | RESOLVED_PRIMARY_LIMITED: no READY/WAIT input exists. SPRU001B requires the TMS32010-20 external master clock to remain within 48.78–150 ns and each pulse within 47.5–52.5% of its period; `CLKOUT` is four input periods, yielding a 195.12–600 ns physical machine-cycle envelope. Bounded slowing inside that envelope is supported, but an arbitrary/indefinite phase pause is outside specified conditions. TI does not separately characterize dynamic modulation between otherwise conforming periods. The tested FPGA `clock_enable_i` hold remains platform-only | closed by SPRU001B §2.12 and Appendix A Clock Characteristics and Timing, printed pp. 2-20 and data-sheet pp. 10–11 (PDF pp. 44 and 366–367); physical testing only if per-cycle modulation matters | a pin-compatible adapter must meet the fixed envelope; a MiSTer emulation may pause internal modeled phases without claiming physical compatibility |

| OQ-003 | What value does `SST` produce in reserved bit 1? | RESOLVED_CORROBORATED: bits 12:9 and 7:2 are unambiguously stored as ones across the original overview, LST, and SST figures; only bit 1 conflicts. | OQ-004 | What is the exact interrupt-recognition and vector-fetch cycle trace? | PARTIALLY RESOLVED_PRIMARY: SPRU001B Figure 2-12 establishes fetch N, fetch N+1, dummy fetch N+2, and fetch vector 2 beside execute N, execute N+1, dummy, and execute 2. | OQ-007 | What are exact taken/untaken branch prefetch, execute-interval, and address traces? | PARTIALLY RESOLVED: TI establishes overlap, two words/two cycles, the following target operand, and ordinary program fetches, but no dedicated original-part branch/call pin waveform has been located. Exact `B`, `BANZ`, `BV`, `BIOZ`, `CALL`, and the six accumulator-conditional branches now have INFERRED explicit-pipeline mappings: opcode-prefetch completion enters execute ownership; PC+1 operand fetch is execution cycle 1; selected-instruction fetch is execution cycle 2; the branch/call retires as that word enters the execute slot. B always selects target. BANZ uses the old selected nine-bit counter to select target/fallthrough and defers its modulo-512 decrement until retirement. The accumulator family selects from the unchanged full 32-bit ACC and preserves it. BV selects from old sticky OV and clears it only at taken retirement. BIOZ samples raw active-low BIO at operand completion and retains the resulting decision, not an opcode-time pin latch, through the selected fetch. CALL selects the target unconditionally and pushes opcode-PC+2 only at selected-target capture. Directed tests cover every integrated condition, target/fallthrough selection, selected-fetch stalls, no early fetched-instruction effect, retirement-only mutations, nested CALL stack shifting, and malformed-operand parking. These combined commit mappings must not be called VERIFIED_PRIMARY. BIOZ's raw active-low sampling fact, CALL's stack/target effects, and all numeric totals remain primary-verified. CALA and RET are functionally/model-qualified as exact words `0x7f8c`/`0x7f8d` with documented state effects and two-cycle totals, but their second external cycles remain UNKNOWN | original TI branch/return control timing, development-system traces, or physical logic-analyzer captures; continue explicit integration one primary-defined family at a time under targeted tests | program trace alignment and interrupt-return timing |

| OQ-011 | Does original-TMS32010 `ADDH` affect `OV`/`OVM`, and if saturated does its low accumulator half change? | RESOLVED_CORROBORATED: SPRU013's instruction-description contract says each Execution block lists affected status and mode behavior, but the original-family ADDH block lists neither.
